What is governance?

Governance gives eligible participants a way to review proposals and express preferences about community decisions. A vote does not automatically guarantee a proposal will be implemented.

Testnet · separate network

Explore voting safely

The community Testnet has been used to explore staking and governance. Testnet balances are not real Community BDAG and have no monetary value.

BDAG Explorer · Mr Wilson

🔐 Unbonded BDAG Claim Tool

Mr Wilson's BDAG Explorer page provides direct access to staking contract actions for unbonding, unstaking and claiming eligible BDAG. It was created to help when a withdrawal could not be completed through the usual interface.

Network: Community BDAG Chain 1404 · Suggested RPC on provider page: rpc.bdagexplorer.com

Wallet safety: This link opens an external service. Connect only the wallet that originally staked, check the network and contract, and read every transaction before approving. Never share your recovery phrase. Claim eligibility and transaction success are not guaranteed.
