23 November 2026 · Official developer meeting summary

Community BDAG protocol open-sourced — governance and Staking V2 update

The BDAG Community developers report that the protocol is now open source and independently community-run. Anyone can inspect the chain software and propose improvements.

  • Open source: Public access to the chain software supports independent review and development.
  • DAO governance: Miners and stakers are intended to participate in protocol decisions through voting power.
  • Upgrade threshold: Developers state that 75% of mining hashpower must support the upgrade for 100,000 blocks.
  • Staking V2: Following successful activation, the new system contracts can be accepted and the associated staking interfaces launched.
  • Community ownership: Developers report an independent network without a single controlling party, and rule out further centrally operated presales.

Status: The article describes the developers’ reported progress and activation requirements; successful Mainnet activation is not independently confirmed here. Exchange listings, a further node release and a post-upgrade snapshot are not confirmed in this meeting summary.

23 November 2026 · Operator update · Activation pending

⛓️ Why the Community BDAG node upgrade matters

Mr Wilson's update: He said his mining pools are already using the new node version, and the network is waiting for around 75% of mining hashpower to run the upgraded software before the change can activate at a coming epoch.

What is it for? The upgrade is intended to move Chain 1404 toward community voting and DAO-controlled staking. That would give the community a way to vote on changes and put staking under DAO governance rather than the control of one individual.

What happens next? More mining operators need to upgrade and the activation conditions must be met. The timing, successful activation and live Mainnet functionality still need to be confirmed on-chain.

This is a dated report of a mining operator's explanation, not independent confirmation that the upgrade or DAO staking is active. Facts first, hype second!
