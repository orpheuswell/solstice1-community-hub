Mobile & PC

📱 How to set it up

  1. PC: Open MetaMask in your browser. Phone: Open the MetaMask app and, if needed, use its built-in browser to visit the RPC setup page.
  2. Visit the community RPC page using the button here.
  3. Tap Add to MetaMask if offered, then review the network name, Chain ID 1404, RPC URL, currency symbol and explorer shown in MetaMask.
  4. Approve only if the details match the trusted community network information.
  5. Switch networks in MetaMask and check your balance or a transaction using an independent explorer.

If the setup button does not work on your device, use MetaMask's Add custom network option and copy the settings from the trusted RPC provider. Do not guess missing settings.

Lesson 7 · RPC explained

🔁 What does an RPC do, and how does automatic failover work?

An RPC is the connection MetaMask uses to ask a blockchain node for information, such as your balance, or to broadcast a transaction. An RPC is not your wallet and does not hold your coins.

🦊 MetaMask→🌐 Community RPC service→⛓️ Healthy Chain 1404 node

Why automatic switching helps: A managed RPC service can check its connected nodes and direct requests away from nodes that are unavailable or behind the network. MetaMask keeps using the same RPC address while the service chooses a suitable backend node. Actual failover depends on the provider's current configuration and health checks; it is not a guarantee of uninterrupted service.

What if an RPC fails? Your balance might not load, transactions might appear delayed or a wallet might show a connection error. That does not automatically mean your BDAG is lost or the whole network is down. Check a trusted independent explorer and the RPC dashboard before trying again.

Safety: Adding a network or changing an RPC does not move your coins. Never enter your secret recovery phrase or private key into an RPC website. Always review MetaMask prompts and transaction approvals.
